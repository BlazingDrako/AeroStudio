Pteronautics

Free, visual aerospace engineering tools that run in your browser. Every result comes with a picture to attach the concept to. No sign-up, nothing to install.

Tools

  • Airfoil Lab: viscous airfoil analysis (an XFOIL-class solver) with live flow visualization, pressure, boundary layer, transition and stall, polar sweeps, supersonic sections and vehicle aerodynamics.
  • Shocks & Nozzles: compressible-flow calculator, normal and oblique shocks, expansion fans, nozzles and plumes, the θ–β–M chart, a shock tube and an Euler CFD solver.
  • Fluid Flow Sandbox: real-time 2-D viscous flow on the GPU (lattice Boltzmann) with vortex streets, stalling wings and obstacles you can draw.
  • Rocket Engines: size a liquid rocket engine from a thrust target with equilibrium chemistry, compare real engines, and explore propellants, plumes and cooling.
  • Jet Engine Cycles: turbojet to scramjet cycle analysis station by station, trade studies, inlets, compressors, turbines and compressor maps.
  • Structures Workbench: statics, 2-D and 3-D frames, 2-D finite element analysis, cross sections, Mohr's circle, vibration and buckling.
  • Flight & Atmosphere: the standard atmosphere, airspeeds, aircraft performance, flight envelope, stability and dynamic modes.
  • Guidance, Navigation & Control: control loops, fly-by-wire, autopilots, navigation and INS/GNSS Kalman filtering.
  • Orbital Mechanics: orbital elements in 3-D, Hohmann and bi-elliptic transfers, plane changes, ground tracks and Δv budgets from the launch pad to Mars.
  • Formula Library: 127 aerospace formulas you can solve for any variable, plot and convert between units.
